# Glossary — One Math Book 2 (grades 10–12) in Simplified Chinese

Written by the coordinator (claude-opus-5-5) on 2026-11-16 for the pilot run
(`sources/TRANSLATION_BOOKS_1-5_ZH.md`). One line per group of English surface
forms: `chapter | English | Chinese`. Chapter `00` means "every chapter whose
English text uses the term" (Book 2 has three years, so chapter numbers repeat).
Use the Chinese term exactly. If a term reads wrong in your sentence, keep it and
rephrase around it: a synonym is never linked, and the reader meets two words
for one notion.
Book 1's lines also apply where a term recurs (fraction 分数, prime number 质数, mean 平均数, median 中位数, range 极差, slope 斜率…).
